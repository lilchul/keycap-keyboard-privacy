Keymew Support

How do I add the keyboard?

Go to Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard and select Keymew. In a text field, touch and hold the globe button to switch to Keymew.

How do I restore a purchase?

Sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account used for the purchase, then tap Restore purchases on the Premium screen in Keymew.
