Keymew Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Keymew processes text composition and cursor movement on your device. Keymew does not send text you type with the keyboard or clipboard content to its servers or analytics services.

Keyboard and information stored on your device

When you type with Keymew in another app, the keyboard may read text around the insertion point to compose characters or move the cursor. Phrases, emoticons and saved items that you choose to save, as well as keyboard settings, are kept on your device. You can delete saved items in the app.

Optional app usage analytics

App usage analytics are off by default. If you consent, the Keymew app starts Google Analytics for Firebase. The keyboard extension does not contain the analytics SDK. You can use themes and the keyboard if you decline.

  • Improving themes and features. The app sends Google information such as which themes you preview or apply, which categories and screens you open, and where sound previews fail. Search queries and the contents of saved phrases are not included in events.
  • Understanding the usage environment. Google processes an installation-specific app identifier, app and operating system versions, device information, session information and an approximate region inferred from the network connection. Events from the same installation can be associated with one another. Precise location and the advertising identifier (IDFA) are not collected, and this data is not used for advertising or tracking across other companies' apps and websites.
  • Retention. Google Analytics user-level and event-level data is retained for two months, without extending the period when new activity occurs. This period does not apply to aggregated reports such as total usage counts. Google's data retention guidance

To withdraw consent, turn off “Share App Analytics” in Keymew's settings. This stops subsequent analytics collection and resets the analytics state on the device. You can request deletion of data already sent by contacting us below.

Theme, dictionary and preview downloads

Six free themes are included in the app. When you download additional themes or language data, or when preview images appear in the gallery, the app requests those files from Cloudflare. The requested resource path, identifier and version, and your connection's IP address are processed. Keyboard input and purchase receipts are not included in file requests.

Requests for theme and language data are rate-limited by IP address to prevent abuse. Keymew has disabled individual file access logging (Data Access Logs) for both the theme-file and preview-image buckets and does not use Logpush to export request logs. Information Cloudflare processes for its own network operations and security is governed by the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Downloaded themes and language data are stored on your device and can be deleted under “Manage downloads” in the app's settings. Preview images are cached temporarily on the device to reduce repeated downloads.

Premium purchases and restoration

Apple processes payments and subscriptions. To restore purchases and determine access to paid themes, Keymew verifies Apple transaction information and stores information such as product type, access expiration and verification time on the device for sharing with the keyboard. The Keymew operator does not receive card numbers or payment method details. Apple Privacy Policy

External services and international processing

Google LLC processes the analytics information described above when you use the app after consenting, and Cloudflare, Inc. processes the download information described above when files are requested over the network. Each provider may process data in countries where it operates servers, including the United States. You can turn off analytics in the app's settings. Download information is processed to provide the requested online files. Firebase Privacy and Security · Cloudflare Privacy Policy

This policy and the support page are hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open a page, GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security. GitHub Pages data collection

Privacy inquiries and requests

To request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of personal information, contact the Keymew operator (pak chul) at guishinexpert@gmail.com. The email address and contents you send will be used to review and respond to your request. Analytics records are not associated with names or email addresses, so it may be difficult to identify records for a particular installation using an email address alone. We will explain what can be verified and the outcome of your request.

Changes to this policy and their effective dates will be announced on this page. Keymew Support
